Privacy

Last updated 17 October 2026

Puzzleth is a free puzzle game. You can play it without an account, and without sending anything about yourself anywhere. Only the online leaderboard, which you have to choose to join, sends data to a server. This page explains what is sent and what happens to it.

Who is responsible

Puzzleth is made and run by Liliana Silvia Huci. For any question or request about your data, write to thealepou@icloud.com.

Playing the game

Your players, progress and settings are saved on your own device (in the browser's storage, or in a file on your computer for the desktop version). They are never sent to us. The game uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, and loads part of its Python runtime from pygame-web.github.io, which GitHub also hosts. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when your browser loads these files. We don't get these logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The online leaderboard

When you press Join on the leaderboard screen, the game sends the following to our server. It is only used to run the leaderboard and let you move to another device.

WhatWhyHow long
Your player name, and a random four-character tag To show you on the leaderboardUntil you leave
Your puzzle ratings, peaks and number of puzzles played To rank youUntil you leave
A backup of your progress and settings To restore them on another deviceUntil you leave
A hash of your login token and of your recovery code To recognise you, without storing the token or code themselvesUntil you leave
A keyed hash of your IP address, when you join or enter a wrong recovery code To limit how many players can be created, and codes guessed, from one address 24 hours

Your IP address itself is never stored. It is hashed with a secret key (HMAC-SHA-256), so the stored value can't be turned back into the address.

The legal basis for the leaderboard data is that you asked to take part in the leaderboard (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). The basis for the 24-hour address records is our legitimate interest in preventing abuse (Article 6(1)(f)).

Your name, tag and ratings are shown publicly to other players on the leaderboard.

Who processes the data

The leaderboard runs on Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1. Cloudflare processes the data on our behalf, and, as with any web request, sees your IP address when the game contacts the server. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Your rights

You can delete everything the leaderboard holds about you at any time: open the leaderboard and choose Leave the leaderboard. Removing your player in the game does the same.

You also have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to how it is used. Write to the address above. If you think we handle your data unlawfully, you can complain to your country's data protection authority.

Children

The leaderboard asks only for a short player name, which needn't be your real name. If you are under the age your country sets for agreeing to online services (between 13 and 16 in the EU), please ask a parent before joining.
