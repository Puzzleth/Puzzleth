## The Beginning
I always admired intelligent people. I wanted to be one of them. I wanted to have a high IQ (Intelligence Quotient). I wanted to be a genius. I wanted to be the best, like no one ever was. 

But every time I would take an IQ test, I would either be entering a panic/anxiety attack right in the middle of the test, be sleep deprived, or both at the same time. More often than not, I would have entered a panic attack before hand that exact day, along with being sleep deprived for several days.

That happened when I was 11 years old. School never taught me what I wanted to learn.

## The Predecessor (Brain-Puzzlerer)
Initially, Puzzleth was named "Brain-Puzzlerer" (yes, that was what I named it, with the typo included) and it was made with the purpose to make me prepared to take an actual IQ test now that I’m an adult. It was never supposed to be public. I made the initial game, and then I asked my coding assistant, Claude, if people would enjoy it if I made it public. It asked me if I envision making the game for researchers, for those nervous like me and with my conditions, or a third option, which I forgot.

## Puzzleth And The Current Day!
Then, Puzzleth was born. I chose to make it public, for those nervous like me.

A day, maybe two later, I shared my project with my cousin, who's a teacher. She told me something I never expected to hear from a teacher. She said "I'll send it to a couple people."

I didn't even ask for her to share it. She just... wanted to. And I was incredibly happy. A teacher sharing an IQ simulator which isn't even supposed to be for scoring? No one would voluntarily share it... would they?

Yet here she was. Encouraging me.

And now I plan on making Puzzleth (the game) a wonderful experience for people who love brain teasers. And I plan on making more edutainment games in the future, all under the Puzzleth brand!

*--Lily*
