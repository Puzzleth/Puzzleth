# Stellara

A new game named [Stellara](https://stellaras.vercel.app/) is live as a prototype!

This game has the funniest backstory. I have recently purchased a subscription to Cursor, which came with Grok Bot. I decided it would be fun to give it access to my email "just in case [The God of Play, Tet,](https://no-game-no-life.fandom.com/wiki/Tet) were to challenge me to a game." What initially started as a funny joke regarding gods from anime evolved into a game.

I told Grok Bot to give me summaries of any interesting emails. Including spam! as that's where the gods of play reside and I might have the chance of getting isekai'd (haha, as if!)

Jokes aside, it is funny to think how an experiment regarding my email inbox turned into a game.

Then Grok Bot found an astrology email (see below).

## Notes I (along with Grok Bot when it found the email) wrote about Stellara:

The astrology email, night of Oct 18 to 19. A newsletter, explained how planets "rule" zodiac signs. Saturn rules Capricorn, an earth sign and his "day house", and Aquarius, an air sign and his "night house". One line stood out: "Capricorn holds through weight. Aquarius holds through pattern." The idea was to turn that structure into puzzle rules, presented as real astronomy, history and mythology rather than as believing in astrology.

First idea, Oct 19 at 5:36 AM. I filed it in an ideas folder, "Planets ruling houses". It was a ring of houses where earth means physics-style rules and air means logic-style rules, plus a day and night switch and "retrograde reshuffles". One open question was whether it should be its own game or a chapter of Puzzleth.

Second idea, 20 minutes later. The retrograde part grew into another idea, "Telescope view vs. system view". You'd look at a confusing telescope view from Earth and a simple top-down view of the solar system, and solve puzzles by matching the two. Fez, Superliminal, Monument Valley and Outer Wilds were the inspirations.

The name, Oct 20 at 2:32 AM. I mentioned my next game, "Stellara", an astronomy edutainment game for Gen Alpha, Beta and Gamma. With my main focus being high school education.
